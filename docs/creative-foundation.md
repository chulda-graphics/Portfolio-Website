# Version 2 Creative Foundation

This document preserves the creative intelligence developed for Dhrex while
deliberately separating it from the retired Version 1 implementation.

## Brand position

Dhrex is a SaaS motion graphics designer working remotely worldwide. The work
exists to make software clearer, easier to understand, and more memorable.
Motion is part of the product experience—not a decorative layer applied after
the thinking is complete.

The portfolio should speak primarily to SaaS founders, CEOs, product teams,
and software companies looking for launch films, UI/product motion, or SaaS
video editing.

## Personality

- Calm and precise
- Purpose-led rather than trend-led
- Minimal, clean, and spacious
- Confident without being loud
- Premium through restraint and detail
- Technically exact but human in tone

## Visual language

- Near-black backgrounds, never flat or visually empty
- Warm off-white foregrounds rather than harsh pure white
- Restrained neutral greys for secondary information
- Fine, low-contrast rules and borders
- Generous negative space
- Modern sans-serif typography only
- DM Sans remains the baseline type choice unless the Version 2 brief replaces it
- Large typography should feel editorial, controlled, and legible
- Visual hierarchy must come from scale, rhythm, spacing, and contrast—not ornament

### Baseline tokens

These are creative reference values, not a frozen final design system:

- Ink: `#050505`
- Surface: `#0a0a0a`
- Primary text: `#f4f4f1`
- Secondary text: `#858580`
- Fine line: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`
- Primary easing character: controlled acceleration with a soft, decisive landing

## Motion philosophy

Every movement must have a job. Motion should guide attention, preserve
context, explain a product, reinforce usability, or create a meaningful
connection between ideas.

The quality bar is premium product motion: exact timing, considered easing,
intentional scene rhythm, precise typography, subtle micro-interactions, and
sound used only when it materially strengthens the experience.

Rich motion is welcome where it improves understanding. It must never create
friction, obscure content, hijack scrolling, or exist only to impress.

## UX principles

1. Clarity before spectacle.
2. Understand the product before animating it.
3. Keep navigation and project discovery immediately understandable.
4. Give case studies dedicated pages with enough space for process and creative decisions.
5. Use horizontal storytelling only in selected sections where it genuinely helps.
6. Treat desktop, tablet, touch, reduced-motion, and keyboard experiences as first-class.
7. Preserve performance budgets from the first implementation decision.
8. Prefer progressive enhancement; core content must remain available without advanced motion.
9. Avoid CV-style presentation. The work, thinking, and outcomes should establish credibility.
10. Use the discovery call as the primary conversion action.

## Creative references

The intended standard is the restraint and product clarity associated with
Apple, Linear, Arc, and Raycast, interpreted into original work for Dhrex.
References should inform quality and principles, never be copied literally.

## Version 2 guardrails

- Do not restore Version 1 code or assets by default.
- Do not recreate the laptop intro, carousel, route transitions, or old case-study system unless the new brief explicitly calls for them.
- Do not assume animation is automatically better than a simpler interaction.
- Do not introduce a visual direction that conflicts with calm precision, monochrome restraint, or purpose-led motion without explicit approval.
